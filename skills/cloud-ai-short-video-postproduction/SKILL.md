# Cloud AI Short-Video Post-Production Skill

## Purpose
Turn an approved short-form video script or rough cut into a clean, retention-focused post-production brief. This skill covers **captions, sound effects (SFX), and background music (BGM) only**. Do not invent footage or replace the creator's visual plan.

## Inputs
Use the material supplied by the creator:
- Platform: Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, or both.
- Target duration and aspect ratio (default: 9:16).
- Final voice-over or dialogue transcript.
- Video/scene timeline, if available.
- Language, audience, and desired emotional tone.
- Any pronunciation, spelling, or religious/cultural wording that must remain exact.

If an input is missing, make conservative assumptions and label them. Do not fabricate facts, quotations, locations, or events.

## Workflow

### 1. Inspect and preserve
- Follow the supplied script and scene order.
- Preserve the speaker's intended meaning and exact names.
- Do not rewrite dialogue unless explicitly asked.
- Do not add claims, extra narration, logos, watermarks, or visual assets.
- Keep the edit understandable with sound muted.

### 2. Captions
- Generate accurate, synchronized captions from the supplied speech.
- Use concise phrase-based line breaks; normally 3–7 words per caption line.
- Keep captions inside mobile-safe margins and away from platform UI.
- Use strong contrast, a clean premium typeface, and consistent styling.
- Highlight only key words; avoid excessive color changes, bouncing, emojis, or all-caps.
- Do not cover faces, important objects, or essential on-screen text.
- Check spelling, punctuation, names, and timing manually where possible.

### 3. Sound effects
- Add SFX only when they reinforce a real beat in the edit: hook, reveal, transition, impact, pause, or ending.
- Keep effects short, clean, and subordinate to speech.
- Use silence intentionally before important reveals.
- Avoid repetitive whooshes, random impacts, meme sounds, and clutter.
- Never use an effect that changes the apparent meaning of a real event.
- Do not add frightening or sensational effects to sensitive religious, tragic, or factual material.

### 4. Background music
- Select music that supports the intended emotion without competing with dialogue.
- Duck music clearly under speech; keep voice intelligible on a phone speaker.
- Use restrained rises and drops to support story beats, not constant intensity.
- Avoid abrupt endings; finish with a deliberate tail or clean cut.
- Use only music and SFX that are licensed, platform-cleared, or supplied by the creator. Do not imply that unlicensed audio is safe to publish.

### 5. Quality control
Before export, verify:
- Captions match every spoken word and are synchronized.
- Voice remains clear above music and effects.
- No clipped peaks, sudden volume jumps, or distracting SFX.
- Caption placement respects safe areas.
- The first 1–2 seconds communicate the hook clearly.
- The ending feels intentional and does not cut off speech.
- Export matches the requested vertical format and duration.

## Output
Return:
1. A brief summary of the caption style.
2. A timestamped SFX cue list.
3. A BGM mood and mixing brief.
4. Any transcript or timing uncertainties that need creator review.
5. Export settings used or recommended.

## Default creative direction
Aim for polished, cinematic, minimal, and emotionally precise. Prioritize clarity and retention over visual noise. Do not imitate a named creator, brand, film, or copyrighted style; translate references into general attributes such as fast pacing, clean typography, suspenseful pauses, or cinematic sound design.

## Important boundary
This skill is an instruction document, not a direct Cloud AI integration. Import or paste it into Cloud AI only if that product supports custom skills or reusable instructions. Never provide account credentials, passwords, OTPs, or API keys to a skill.
